2. **吧台近景**
   Close-up of a worn wooden saloon bar counter, whiskey bottles and shot glasses, brass foot rail, scratched varnish, a cloth rag, oil lamp unlit, warm side light from a window, shallow depth of field

3. **自动钢琴**
   An old upright player piano in a frontier saloon, paper roll visible behind a small glass door, keys moving by themselves, chipped dark wood, late afternoon light, dust particles in the air

4. **黄昏 + 油灯**
   The same frontier saloon at dusk, orange sunset light through windows mixed with warm oil lamps on tables, long shadows, a lone stranger silhouette in the doorway backlit, tense atmosphere

5. **重置瞬间（风格探索）**
   Frontier saloon interior frozen in time, desaturated cold teal tones, dust particles suspended mid-air, light shafts frozen, uncanny stillness, subtle film burn at the edges
